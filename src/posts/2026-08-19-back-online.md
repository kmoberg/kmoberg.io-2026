date: 2026-08-19

This blog used to run on Ghost, in a container, in a DMZ, behind a Cloudflare Tunnel — right up until the tunnel quietly died and took the whole thing with it.

It now runs as a static site: markdown files in a git repo, built with Eleventy, deployed automatically on push. Nothing to patch, nothing to tunnel, nothing to collapse at 2 AM.

Old posts are on their way back as they get migrated over.
